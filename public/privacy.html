Privacy Policy

Center for Adventure Leadership (CAL)

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 · https://adventureleader.org

1. Introduction & Scope

The Center for Adventure Leadership (CAL) is a nonprofit educational organization whose mission is to train adults to be safe and effective adventure leaders for youth.

This Privacy Policy applies across all services provided by the Center for Adventure Leadership, including:

  • Our website (https://adventureleader.org);
  • Our in-person and digital training courses, workshops, and certifications (such as Wilderness First Aid);
  • Our mobile and web applications, including TrailSafe and any future applications developed or distributed by CAL; and
  • Any communications, newsletters, or direct inquiries between you and CAL.

Whether you are participating in a wilderness course, visiting our website, or using our mobile apps on the trail, we believe outdoor education and safety tools must respect your privacy by design.

2. Our Core Privacy Principles

  • Privacy by Architecture: We minimize the collection of personal data across all our platforms. Where services can operate locally on your device without transmitting data to a server, we build them to do so.
  • No Sale or Renting of Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties, advertising brokers, or data aggregators. Ever.
  • No Commercial Ad Tracking: We do not host commercial advertisements, install tracking pixels, or use behavioral advertising networks across our website or applications.
  • Transparency & Control: You have direct control over your information, including the ability to inspect, export, or delete your data.

3. Information Collected via Our Website & Educational Programs

A. Course Registrations & Inquiries

When you register for a class (such as Wilderness First Aid), sign up for workshops, or submit an inquiry via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, organization or troop affiliation (if applicable), and any course-specific details you share.

Payments: Course payments are processed securely through established third-party payment processors (such as Stripe or Squarespace Payments). CAL does not receive, process, or store your full credit card numbers or banking credentials.

Training Records: We maintain internal records of completed courses, certifications issued, and course dates so we can verify credentials, issue renewal reminders, or confirm student participation.

B. Website Analytics & Technical Logs

Like most standard websites, our hosting servers collect basic technical access logs (such as IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and access timestamps) to maintain server security, diagnose operational issues, and monitor site reliability.

Our website uses essential functional cookies required for website operation and secure form submission. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Mobile Applications Policy

The Center for Adventure Leadership builds mobile applications to support outdoor safety, trip planning, and leader readiness.

A. General Standards for All CAL Mobile Apps

Every mobile application published by CAL adheres to the following baseline standards:

  • Local-First Data Storage: User data (trip plans, checklists, notes, calculations, user profiles) defaults to local storage on your device. We do not maintain central cloud databases of your trip activities.
  • Explicit Permissions: We request device permissions (such as location or storage) only when strictly required for core functionality that you initiate. Permissions are never used for background tracking or behavioral profiling.
  • No Commercial Analytics or Ad Networks: Our production mobile apps contain no behavioral advertising trackers, advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID), or commercial telemetry SDKs.
  • Offline Capability: Whenever possible, our apps are engineered to function completely offline without active cellular or internet connectivity.
  • No Automatic Emergency Dispatch: CAL mobile apps are educational aids and preparation tools; they are not automated monitoring or dispatch systems.

B. TrailSafe Application Disclosures

App Specific: TrailSafe

For users of our TrailSafe hiking preparation and emergency companion app (iOS, Android, and Web), the following specific privacy practices apply:

  • Location Data (GPS):
    • Foreground-Only Access: TrailSafe requests foreground location permission (When In Use) solely to determine your current GPS coordinates while viewing screens that explicitly calculate or display location information (Emergency, Daylight/Sun tool, and Coordinate display).
    • Transient In-Memory Processing: Coordinates are processed ephemerally in volatile memory. Location fixes are never attached to saved trip plans, saved to persistent local storage, or transmitted to CAL servers.
    • Zero Background Tracking: The app explicitly blocks background location services (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION are disabled). GPS hardware is deactivated immediately when you navigate away or close the app.
  • Trip Plans, Checklists, & User Profile:
    • All trip details (destinations, route descriptions, turnaround times, check-in deadlines, party sizes, vehicle details, trusted contacts) and preparation checklist progress are stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage).
    • You do not need to register or create an account to use any feature of TrailSafe.
  • Emergency Communications Handoffs:
    • Call 911: Tapping "CALL 911" passes the number 911 to your device’s native telephone dialer (tel:911). The call is not placed automatically; you must confirm and dial in the native phone app.
    • Text 911: Tapping "TEXT 911" opens your device’s native SMS composer with a pre-filled draft containing your coordinates (or the last known location of a missing person) and emergency details. You review, edit, and send the message yourself.
    • PDF & Plan Sharing: Sharing a trip plan generates a local document and invokes your device's native OS share sheet (e.g., Mail, Messages, AirDrop). Data is shared only with the specific recipients you choose.
  • Local Notifications: Optional return-time and overdue check-in reminders are scheduled strictly as local on-device notifications. TrailSafe does not use remote push notifications (APNs or FCM).
  • Production Diagnostics: Final store releases have all diagnostic and crash reporting SDKs (including Bugsnag) completely disabled and stripped from the binary. Pre-release testing builds (TestFlight) may enable Bugsnag to catch native crashes, with personal data and identifiers strictly redacted.

C. Future Applications & Features

If CAL introduces new applications or optional cloud-connected features in the future, cloud synchronization will always be strictly optional and opt-in, with advance notice and governed by our strict no-ad-tracking principles.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We disclose information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: We work with trusted service providers who assist with operations (website hosting, email communications, and payment processing). They have access only to the data necessary to perform their functions and are bound by confidentiality.
  • Legal & Safety: When required by law or to protect life, health, or safety in an emergency situation.
  • User-Directed Sharing: When you choose to share a trip plan, send an emergency message, or export a file from our apps, data is transferred directly to the recipients you designate.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Websites use TLS/HTTPS encryption, and mobile applications rely on sandboxed on-device operating system encryption.

Your Data Control: In TrailSafe, tapping Delete all local data in the About screen permanently wipes all stored trip plans, profile details, and checklist progress. Uninstalling any CAL mobile app removes all associated local application files.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

CAL focuses on training adult adventure leaders, mentors, and educators. Our services and mobile applications are directed to individuals who are at least 13 years old (and typically adults 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Updates & Contact

Any revisions to this policy will be posted at https://adventureleader.org/privacy with an updated effective date.
